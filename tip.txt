Golden Rule 🔥

Time ka lower bound problem batati hai.

Space ka lower bound algorithm batata hai.

Isliye experienced programmers jab question padhte hain, turant do cheezein sochte hain:

Can I do it in-place? → Agar haan, target O(1) SC.
Agar nahi, minimum kya store karna padega? → Usi se SC ka andaaza lagta hai.

Ye intuition practice se aati hai. Tum jitne zyada DSA questions karoge, utna jaldi question dekhte hi bol paoge: "Iska O(1) space solution possible hai" ya "Nahi, isme O(n) extra space unavoidable lag rahi hai."
