now tc and sc of binary search
lets say i have collection of size N
N > N/2 > N/2^2 > N/2^3 > N/2^4 ..... > N/2^k
at N/2^k array size became 1

N/2^k = 1
N = 2 ^ k
taking log2n both side
log2(N)=k
k steps taken (k eliminations)
tc O(logN) O(log2N)
sc O(1)

so can we see a pattern

calculate mid
compare
shrink search space
repeat

repated works can be done by recurrsion
(good for just recurssion practice not good for use as extra stack space O(logN) as k for k steps log2n func call take place so)
